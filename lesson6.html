6. Advanced Math
Introduction: The Tricky Operators

You know how to add, subtract, multiply, and divide. But Python has three more math symbols that are incredibly powerful and used all the time in programming.

Let's look at Floor Division, Remainder, and Powers.

Floor Division (//)

Imagine you have 7 oranges and you want to share them equally among 2 friends. You can't give them 3.5 oranges each. You give them 3 each, and 1 is left over.

The // operator gives you the whole number share, dropping the decimal completely.

print(7 // 2) # Output: 3

Remainder (%)

Remember those 7 oranges shared among 2 friends? The % operator tells you what is left over (the remainder).

print(7 % 2) # Output: 1

This is super useful for checking if a number is even or odd! (If x % 2 == 0, it's even).

🎯 Powers (**)

If you want to multiply a number by itself multiple times, you use ** (two asterisks).

For example, 2 to the power of 3 (2 × 2 × 2):

print(2 ** 3) # Output: 8

BODMAS (Order of Operations)

Python follows the exact same math rules you learned in school: Brackets, Orders (Powers), Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction.

print(2 + 3 * 4) # Output: 14 (Not 20!)
print((2 + 3) * 4) # Output: 20

🎯 Quick Summary

• // gives the whole number share.

• % gives the remainder.

• ** gives the power.

• Python follows BODMAS. Use brackets ( ) to control the order.
