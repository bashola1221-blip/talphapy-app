1. Your First Python Program
Part 1: What is Python?

Imagine you want to build a house. You can't just tell a builder "make me a house" — you have to give clear instructions, one step at a time.

A programming language works the same way. You give the computer clear instructions, one line at a time. Python is one of those languages.

Example:

print("Hello, world!")

That line tells the computer: show the words "Hello, world!" on the screen.

Think of a market announcer with a megaphone. He shouts: "Fresh tomatoes!" — everyone hears it.

print() is Python's megaphone. Whatever you put inside it, Python shouts to the screen.

print("My name is Ada")
print("I am learning Python")

Each print() is a new shout on a new line.

🎯 The one rule: Python is case-sensitive, like your bank password.

print("Hello") # This works ✅
Print("Hello") # This fails ❌

Same letters, but the capital P confuses Python.
