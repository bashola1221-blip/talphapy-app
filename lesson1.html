1. Your First Python Program
Part 1: What is Python?

Imagine you want to build a house. You can't just tell a builder "make me a house" — you have to give clear instructions, one step at a time.

A programming language works the same way. You give the computer clear instructions, one line at a time. Python is one of those languages.

Example:

print("Hello, world!")

That line tells the computer: show the words "Hello, world!" on the screen.
